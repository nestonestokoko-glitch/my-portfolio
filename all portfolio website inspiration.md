# Ultimate Website Research, Design & Development Brief

Before writing any code, conduct an extensive design research process across **2,000 websites** from different industries, categories, and design styles. Study their strongest design patterns, layouts, UI components, visual identities, and user experiences. Your goal is to use this research to create an exceptional, modern, polished, and highly professional website that stands out from ordinary websites.

## 1. Research the Entire Design Landscape

Analyze as many of the 2,000 websites as possible, using available browsing and research tools. Study their:

* Overall layout, page structure, and visual hierarchy.
* Headers, navigation bars, menus, and announcement bars.
* Hero sections, headlines, CTAs, and background treatments.
* Grid systems, columns, spacing, alignment, and content density.
* Color palettes, gradients, contrast, and brand consistency.
* Typography, font pairings, font sizes, and readability.
* Cards, buttons, forms, icons, borders, shadows, and animations.
* Image placement, illustrations, videos, and visual storytelling.
* Footers, multi-column layouts, links, and secondary navigation.
* Responsive behavior across desktop, tablet, and mobile.
* Micro-interactions, hover effects, transitions, and scrolling behavior.
* Accessibility, usability, performance, and conversion-focused design.

## 2. Identify the Best Design Patterns

Do not blindly copy individual websites. Identify the strongest ideas, reusable patterns, and creative approaches across the research. Compare them, understand why they work, and combine the best elements into one cohesive design system tailored to my website's purpose, audience, and brand.

## 3. Build a Complete Design System

Before implementation, establish a consistent visual direction covering:

* Primary, secondary, accent, background, and surface colors.
* Typography scale and font hierarchy.
* Spacing system, grid structure, and container widths.
* Buttons, links, cards, inputs, badges, and reusable components.
* Border radii, shadows, gradients, and visual effects.
* Navigation, headers, footers, and page section patterns.
* Responsive breakpoints and mobile-specific layouts.
* Interaction states, transitions, and accessibility standards.

## 4. Design Every Part of the Website

Treat the entire website as one connected experience. Carefully design every page and section, including the header, navigation, hero, content sections, feature grids, cards, testimonials, pricing tables, forms, calls to action, and footer. Do not neglect small components or leave sections looking unfinished.

Make deliberate decisions about spacing, alignment, proportions, typography, color balance, and visual rhythm. Every section should feel intentional and belong to the same design system.

## 5. Go Beyond Generic AI Website Design

Avoid predictable templates, repetitive card grids, excessive gradients, unnecessary glassmorphism, generic layouts, and decorative effects without purpose. Use originality, strong composition, thoughtful whitespace, excellent typography, and distinctive visual details to create a memorable result.

## 6. Implement and Refine

After researching and establishing the design direction:

1. Plan the site structure and component hierarchy.
2. Implement the complete design using clean, reusable, maintainable code.
3. Ensure every interactive element works correctly.
4. Test layouts across screen sizes and fix overflow, spacing, and alignment issues.
5. Review the complete website visually, section by section.
6. Refine any weak, inconsistent, or unfinished areas.
7. Check accessibility, loading performance, and usability.

## 7. Follow a Research-Driven Standard

Use the 2,000 websites as a broad inspiration and benchmarking library, not as a claim that every website has been inspected if the available tools cannot support that. Track the references actually reviewed, extract useful patterns, and prioritize the most relevant examples.

**Final objective:** Create a world-class website that combines the strongest ideas from a broad range of excellent websites into a unique, cohesive, production-ready experience. Every detail, from the header and grid system to the color palette, typography, responsive layout, interactions, and footer, must feel carefully considered. Do not stop at making the website functional. Make it visually exceptional, intuitive, distinctive, and polished.
